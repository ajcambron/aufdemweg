# Auf dem Weg

German Sunday School at DSB Hall, 2026–27. High schoolers, one mixed class from A1 beginners to students near C1, two hours a week.

Each week has one page written for whoever is teaching that Sunday, whether that's the regular instructor or a parent filling in. The page covers the run of the class, a grammar refresher, the week's vocabulary, the student packet to print, and the answer key. If you're new here, read [Programm & Ablauf]({{ '/programm/' | relative_url }}) first. It takes five minutes and explains how every week works.
