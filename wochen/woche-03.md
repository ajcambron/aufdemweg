# Woche 3 | Familie
October 11, 2026
{: .fs-5 .text-grey-dk-000 }

## Auf einen Blick

| A1 can-do | B1/B2 can-do |
| :--- | :--- |
| Name my family members and give their ages | Describe relationships with family members and give reasons |

**Kernaufgabe:** Familienbaum. Partner A describes their family out loud, one person at a time. Partner B draws and labels a family tree from what they hear, without looking at A's paper. They compare, fix mistakes, then switch. A1/A2 give names and ages; B1/B2 add one relationship comment per person (*Ich verstehe mich gut mit meiner Schwester, weil sie lustig ist*).

{: .note }
Students may describe an **invented or celebrity family** instead of their own. Say this out loud when you introduce the task. Some students won't want to map their real family for a partner, and the language practice is identical either way.

## Grammatik

**Possessives: *mein, meine, meinen*.** Which form depends on the noun's gender and its job in the sentence.

| | der-words (masculine) | das-words (neuter) | die-words (feminine, plural) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Subject: *Das ist ...* | **mein** Vater | **mein** Kind | **meine** Mutter, **meine** Eltern |
| Object: *Ich habe / finde ...* | **meinen** Vater | **mein** Kind | **meine** Mutter, **meine** Eltern |

The only form that changes is the masculine one: *mein* becomes *meinen* when the person is the object of the verb. *Mein Bruder ist 12. Ich finde **meinen** Bruder nervig.* The same goes for *dein, sein, ihr*.

B1/B2 also meet **mit + dative** as a fixed phrase: *Ich verstehe mich gut mit **meinem** Bruder / mit **meiner** Schwester.* Teach it as a chunk; there's no need to unpack the dative this week.

{: .warning }
**What students will do:** say *"Ich habe mein Bruder."* At A1 let it go; the meaning is clear and the ending will come with more input. With B1/B2 in structured output, point at the table above: masculine, object, add *-en*.

The "Accusative" note under the sentence builder in the packet mixes in the dative *mit* phrase. Go by this table if a student asks.

## Wortschatz

| Deutsch | English |
| :--- | :--- |
| Es gibt ... Personen in meiner Familie. | There are ... people in my family. |
| meine Mutter / mein Vater / meine Eltern | my mother / my father / my parents |
| meine Schwester / mein Bruder / meine Geschwister | my sister / my brother / my siblings |
| meine Oma (Großmutter) / mein Opa (Großvater) | my grandma / my grandpa |
| meine Tante / mein Onkel | my aunt / my uncle |
| meine Kusine / mein Cousin | my cousin (f / m) |
| Sie / Er ist ... Jahre alt. | She / He is ... years old. |
| Ich verstehe mich (nicht so) gut mit ... *(B1/B2)* | I get along (not so) well with ... |
| ..., weil er / sie lustig / nett / nervig ist. | ..., because he / she is funny / nice / annoying. |
| die Stiefschwester / der Stiefbruder | stepsister / stepbrother |
| die Patchwork-Familie | blended family |
