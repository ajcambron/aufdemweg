# Abschlussfeier
May 2, 2027 · DSB Hall
{: .fs-5 .text-grey-dk-000 }

## Auf einen Blick

The closing ceremony and showcase, not a packet day. Groups perform the pieces they prepared in [Week 26]({{ '/wochen/woche-26/' | relative_url }}) for families and guests, and the year ends with thanks and a goodbye.

{: .important }
Before guests arrive, check every group: who's here, who's missing, and whether lines need to move. A group missing a member can give that person's lines to someone else or cut them. Nobody should have to perform a part they didn't rehearse.

## Ablauf

| Time | What | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 0:00–0:20 | Ankommen & Probe | Students arrive early. Each group runs its piece once, quietly, and checks props. |
| 0:20–0:30 | Begrüßung | Two students (B1/B2 volunteers) welcome guests in German and English: *Herzlich willkommen, liebe Eltern und Gäste!* |
| 0:30–1:10 | Auftritte | Groups perform in the agreed order. Between acts, a student announces the next group: *Und jetzt: Gruppe 2 mit einem Sketch!* |
| 1:10–1:25 | Rückblick | A short slideshow or poster walk of the year's themes; optional: read a few anonymous *Am besten hat mir ... gefallen* sentences from Week 26. |
| 1:25–1:35 | Dank & Urkunden | Thank-yous and certificates or small awards (best costume at Karneval, best actor in the Christmas skit ...). |
| 1:35–2:00 | Feiern | Snacks, music from the Week 25 class playlist, conversation with guests. |

**Materials:** the performance order on paper, a speaker, any props, certificates if the program uses them, and a sign-in sheet if the hall needs one.

## Moderation (for the student hosts)

| Deutsch | English |
| :--- | :--- |
| Herzlich willkommen, liebe Eltern und Gäste! | A warm welcome, dear parents and guests! |
| Heute zeigen wir, was wir dieses Jahr gelernt haben. | Today we'll show you what we learned this year. |
| Und jetzt: Gruppe ... mit einem Sketch / einem Lied! | And now: group ... with a skit / a song! |
| Applaus, bitte! | A round of applause, please! |
| Vielen Dank fürs Zuhören! | Thank you for listening! |
| Schöne Sommerferien und bis zum nächsten Jahr! | Have a great summer, and see you next year! |

## Feedback

Today is for applause, not correction. If you give feedback at all, make it one specific compliment per group (*Ihr wart so laut und deutlich! Die Idee war super lustig!*). Save notes about language for next year's planning.

## After the ceremony

If students wrote letters to their future selves in the Week 26 extension, collect and store them to hand back next fall. Same for the Week 12 Vorsatz wall, if you kept it.
